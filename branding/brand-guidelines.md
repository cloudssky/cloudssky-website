# CSG Signature V1 — Brand Foundation

**Company:** Clouds Sky GmbH  
**Primary brand:** CSG  
**Supporting wordmark:** CLOUDS SKY  
**Brand statement:** WE DO.

## Design principles

- Minimal, precise, confident, durable.
- CSG is the primary brand; the full company name remains legible.
- No cloud pictogram. The engineering identity is broader than cloud infrastructure.
- Use electric cyan sparingly for the G and the tapered line accents.
- Keep the brand statement separate from the core monogram so the mark works at small sizes.
- No technology-buzzword tagline locked into the logo.

## Core colors

| Name | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Night | `#080E19` | Preferred dark background |
| Titanium White | `#FFFFFF` | C, S, supporting text |
| Electric Cyan | `#00D8EA` | G and fine line accents |
| Sky Blue | `#00A6ED` | Optional gradient endpoint |

## Status and production notes

This is an initial **SVG reconstruction** of the approved visual direction, not an exact vector trace of the earlier image concept. The letterforms and spacing require optical review before trademark, print, or external brand distribution. Avoid treating this draft as a final master asset.

To finish the brand kit, create outlined path-based master vectors, a reversed light-background logo, monochrome versions, and exported PNG sizes. Verify small-screen legibility and contrast.
